🌟 Hands-On Discovery Classroom

Welcome to Our School

Cultivating Confident, Lifelong Learners

Dr. Maria Montessori observed that children learn best through self-directed activity, hands-on learning, and collaborative play.

At Little Explorers, our child-prepared environment allows students to choose work that ignites their curiosity — from tracing sandpaper Devanagari characters (क ख ग घ) to counting golden beads (1 2 3 4) and building English phonics vocabulary (A B C D).

✓
Self-Paced Learning: Children progress according to their individual developmental milestones.
✓
Bilingual Literacy: Master both English alphabet and Devanagari script naturally.
✓
Certified Montessori Teachers: Dedicated educators facilitating independent exploration.
🎨

Creative Expression

Painting, sensory crafts, music, Devanagari calligraphy, and creative drama.

🔢

Concrete Math

Counting blocks 1-20, golden beads, place-value decimal system manipulation.

🔤

Phonics & Script

Sandpaper letters A B C D and क ख ग घ for tactile motor-memory tracing.

🌱

Practical Life

Pouring, gardening, washing, sorting, and courtesy & grace social practice.
